Masala Chai (Spiced Assam & Botanical Infusion)

Robust, comforting Indian spiced tea simmered with green cardamom, Ceylon cinnamon, organic ginger root, cloves, and black pepper.

Extraction Ratio
1 : 25
Target Water Temp
208°F (98°C)

Step-by-Step Extraction Phases

  1. Phase 1: Spice & Leaf Decoction Simmer (240s) — Boil crushed spices and tea leaves in water for 4 minutes to extract essential oils.
  2. Phase 2: Milk & Sweetener Rest (120s) — Add milk and unrefined cane sugar (Panela/Jaggery); bring to gentle froth.
  3. Phase 3: Fine Mesh Strain (30s) — Pour through fine sieve into mug.
