Golden Turmeric Botanical Tea (Herbal Infusion)

Caffeine-free wellness infusion combining golden turmeric root, ginger, lemongrass, black pepper (for curcumin absorption), and raw honey.

Extraction Ratio
1 : 40
Target Water Temp
208°F (98°C)

Step-by-Step Extraction Phases

  1. Phase 1: Botanical Hydration (15s) — Place crushed turmeric and botanicals into infuser.
  2. Phase 2: Deep Botanical Steep (300s) — Steep 5 minutes with boiling 98°C water for rich golden extraction.
  3. Phase 3: Honey & Citrus Stir (30s) — Stir in raw honey and squeeze of fresh lemon.
