Earl Grey Tea (Italian Bergamot Oil Infused)

Aromatic black tea infused with natural cold-pressed oil of Italian bergamot citrus. Fragrant floral citrus aroma balanced with malty cocoa depth.

Extraction Ratio
1 : 50
Target Water Temp
203°F (95°C)

Step-by-Step Extraction Phases

  1. Phase 1: Vessel Warm (15s) — Rinse ceramic teapot with hot water.
  2. Phase 2: Aromatic Citrus Steep (210s) — Steep untouched at 95°C for 3.5 minutes to release citrus aromatics.
  3. Phase 3: Decant (30s) — Separate tea leaves from liqueur.
