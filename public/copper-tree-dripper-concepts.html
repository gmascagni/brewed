🛠️ How to Craft or Commission One: Simple 3-Step Recipe

1

The Wood Base

Source a live-edge black walnut offcut or turned circular block from a local woodworker or Rockler/Woodcraft ($15–$30). Drill a 1/2" or 3/8" blind mounting hole, seal with food-grade Danish oil or beeswax.

2

Bending the Copper Tubing

Use standard soft annealed copper refrigeration tubing (available at Home Depot/Lowe's for $18). Pack the inside with fine sand or use a spring pipe-bender to prevent kinking as you hand-wrap it around a tapered wooden fence post or cylindrical mandrel.

3

Forming the Top Halo Ring

Bend the top into a 75mm – 78mm outer diameter ring (the exact size to cradle a Hario V60 02, Chemex funnel, or Origami dripper). Secure the base with epoxy and buff the copper to a brilliant raw shine or seal with Renaissance Wax.
